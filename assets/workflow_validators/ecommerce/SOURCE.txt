validate_storyboard_package.py is the local ecommerce reference-video workflow
validator distributed with ComicFlow to validate complete delivery folders and ZIPs.
Source: Copy Ecommerce Reference Videos, scripts/validate_storyboard_package.py.
ComicFlow recognizes translated-dialogue headings only at the start of a
field so that source dialogue quoting those words stays valid.
The grayscale check uses pixel-level channel differences so that balanced
colors do not cancel one another when averaged.
